BEST VALUE THIS WEEK

Tap a row to pick it. "Getting" is how many points better the pool's number is than the market. 3 means that gap crosses a key number, which is worth far more than a normal half point. The percentage follows your risk setting: Safe prices against the least favourable book, Swing against the best, Normal splits them. The smaller numbers under it are the floor and ceiling across books. Green + and red − are schedule spots for and against your side. Notes below them are plain context, counted neither way, because a note can help either team.

BOARD

Same layout as the pool: favourite left, underdog right, home team bold and underlined, pool's number in the middle. Tap a team to pick it. Mkt is what the favourite on the left is giving at the sportsbooks. Type over it with Sunday's number and everything reprices. Edited numbers get an orange border. Clear the box to go back to the saved number. Est is the approximate cover chance on the value side, from historical margin frequencies.

SEASON

Graded against the pool's lines. Quarters: 1–4, 5–9, 10–13, 14–18.

SPOT TRACKER

Every flagged spot, logged against how that team actually did versus the pool's line. Fills in as weeks are graded. Don't lean on any row until it has 40+ games. Before that it's noise.
Data sources
